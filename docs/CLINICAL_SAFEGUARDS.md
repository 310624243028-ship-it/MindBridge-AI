# MindBridge AI: Clinical Safeguards, Ethics & Regulatory Boundary

## 1. Non-Diagnostic Regulatory Disclosure

> [!CAUTION]
> **MANDATORY CLINICAL DISCLAIMER**:
> MindBridge AI is an **investigational cognitive screening and longitudinal research instrument**. It is **NOT** a certified medical diagnostic device under FDA 21 CFR Part 860, EU MDR 2017/745, or equivalent medical device regulations. 
> 
> - MindBridge AI does **NOT** diagnose Alzheimer's disease, Frontotemporal Dementia, Lewy Body Dementia, Vascular Dementia, or Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI).
> - Scores generated by this platform are standardized psychometric indicators intended to assist qualified medical professionals in longitudinal monitoring and research data collection.
> - A clinical diagnosis of cognitive impairment or neurodegenerative disease requires comprehensive medical evaluation, including neuroimaging (MRI/PET), cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) or blood biomarkers (p-tau217, Aβ42/40), neurological physical exams, and formal neuropsychological battery testing.

## 2. Separation of Experimental Tasks vs. Validated Clinical Instruments

To maintain psychometric integrity and regulatory compliance, MindBridge AI strictly distinguishes between:

### A. Experimental Cognitive Paradigms (Included in MindBridge AI)
These are open-access, computerized adaptations of classic psychological research paradigms designed for sensitive millisecond reaction time tracking and longitudinal variance analysis:
1. **Computerized Free & Delayed Word Recall**:
   - Tests episodic verbal memory encoding and retrieval delay.
   - Licensing: Open experimental paradigm.
2. **Adaptive Digit Span (Forward & Backward)**:
   - Tests attentional span and working memory manipulation.
   - Licensing: Open cognitive psychology paradigm.
3. **Stroop Color-Word Interference Task**:
   - Tests inhibitory control, cognitive flexibility, and executive function.
   - Licensing: Classic Stroop (1935) paradigm (public domain).
4. **Computerized Trail Making Variant (B-format)**:
   - Alternating number-letter switching for processing speed and executive flexibility.
   - Licensing: Open research variant; not identical to proprietary army individual test battery.
5. **Timed Category Verbal Fluency**:
   - Semantic retrieval under speeded conditions.
   - Licensing: Standard psycholinguistic research task.

### B. Proprietary Validated Clinical Instruments (Documentation & Licensing Notes)
The following commercial/clinical instruments are **NOT** redistributed without vendor agreements:
- **MoCA (Montreal Cognitive Assessment)**: Requires formal clinician training/certification and licensing agreement from MoCA Test Inc. (Z. Nasreddine). Not duplicated as raw unlicensed software.
- **MMSE (Mini-Mental State Examination)**: Copyrighted by Psychological Assessment Resources (PAR).
- **ADAS-Cog**: Standardized for clinical drug trials; requires licensed test kits and protocol certification.

MindBridge AI provides integration APIs for clinicians to manually record external validated scores (e.g., MoCA total score) alongside MindBridge longitudinal experimental time-series data.

## 3. Machine Learning Model Safeguards & Ethics

1. **Research Benchmark Labeling**: All machine learning outputs (such as longitudinal risk progression index) are explicitly tagged in the UI and data layer with:
   - `model_scope: "RESEARCH_INVESTIGATIONAL_ONLY"`
   - `is_diagnostic: false`
2. **Evaluation Metrics Reporting**:
   - All models are evaluated against ROC-AUC, Sensitivity, Specificity, Positive Predictive Value (Precision), Recall, and Brier Calibration Score.
   - Subgroup fairness analysis is required across age brackets and education tiers to detect algorithmic disparity.
3. **Synthetic Data Policy**:
   - Synthetic cohorts are utilized strictly for unit testing, system verification, and interface demonstration.
   - Synthetic cohorts are **NEVER** cited as clinical evidence of diagnostic efficacy.
   - All synthetic records are permanently watermarked with `is_synthetic_benchmark: true`.

## 4. Privacy, Security & Data Minimization

- **Pseudonymization by Default**: Participant names and personal identifiers are decoupled from cognitive test results. Participants are identified solely by a generated pseudonymous code (e.g., `MB-P-7281`).
- **Cryptographic Informed Consent**: Every participant session requires recorded informed consent with timestamp, consent version ID, and cryptographic hash verification.
- **Audit Logging**: Every access to participant records, screening histories, and exported clinical reports is permanently recorded in the `AUDIT_LOGS` table with actor user ID, action type, resource ID, and timestamp.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**:
  - `Participant`: Can only view their own active tests, personal consent records, and personal trends.
  - `Clinician`: Can view assigned participant reports, longitudinal trends, and export screening summaries.
  - `Researcher`: Access to de-identified cohort analytics, model evaluation metrics, and demographic distributions.
  - `Admin`: System health, Exasol database connectivity, and audit logs.
